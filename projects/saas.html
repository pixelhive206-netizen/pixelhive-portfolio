FAQ

Quick answers.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. Our Starter plan is free forever and doesn't require any payment information. You only add a card if you upgrade.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Every paid plan includes branded custom domains. We handle SSL certificates and DNS verification automatically.

Will my old short links keep working?

Always. Once a link is created, it stays live as long as your account is active — even on the free plan.

Yes. Email us at hello@linq.example with proof of status and we'll set you up with a 50% discount on any paid plan.

How do I cancel?

One click in your dashboard. No phone calls, no questions. Your account drops back to the free plan and your links keep working.
