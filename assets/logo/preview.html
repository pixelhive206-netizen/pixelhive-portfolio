What you're trading vs the references you sent

  • Drops the 3D bevel — that's the #1 thing that telegraphs "logo template." Studios use flat marks; agencies use bevels.
  • Drops the inner cube — too literal, makes the mark busy. The hex alone reads as "hive cell" without help.
  • Drops the tagline ("crafting digital experiences") — that's a body-copy element, not a logo element. Live separately on the site.
  • Keeps the pixel dispersion — but distilled to 3 squares of decreasing size, so the gesture is clear at any scale (a Fiverr logo with 20 pixels reads as noise at 24px favicon).
  • Keeps the hex — the strongest semantic move (hive cell ⇄ hexagon ⇄ pixel cluster).
  • One color, scales clean — works at 16px, works at 1080px, works in print, works embroidered.
