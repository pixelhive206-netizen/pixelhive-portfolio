What changed vs v1 (and what's still SVG-correct)

  • Pixels moved inside the hex — your reference had them descending zigzag inside the hive. Reads as more contained, no parts floating outside the boundary.
  • Pixels are rounded (rx=2) — softer feel, matches the rounded square corners in your reference image.
  • Wordmark = PIXEL·HIVE — split with a gold center-dot separator. Makes the pixel/hive concept explicit in the type.
  • STUDIO subline — gold, wide-tracked (9px). Confident "we are a studio" identifier without being a tagline.
  • Stroke width scales — at 16px favicon the hex stroke is heavier (8px), at 100px it's thinner (4.5px). Otherwise tiny faviconswould break the outline.
  • Single-color palette — gold-on-navy primary. The mark uses currentColor so anywhere you drop it, the surrounding text color drives it.
