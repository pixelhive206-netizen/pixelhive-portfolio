FAQ

Common questions.

If your question isn't here, just email us — we usually reply within a few hours.

How long does a project take?

A Starter site usually takes 7 days. A Standard multi-page site takes about 2 weeks. Custom projects are scoped individually — we'll give you an honest timeline before we start.

What's included in each package?

Design, development, mobile-responsive build, basic SEO, and contact form setup. Domain registration and hosting are billed separately (we'll help you set them up). 30 days of free tweaks are included on Standard and Custom packages.

How many revisions do I get?

Up to two rounds of design revisions are included in every package. Beyond that, additional changes are billed at a flat hourly rate — but most clients never need them.

Do you handle hosting and domain?

We don't host websites ourselves, but we'll guide you through setting up a domain and hosting (we usually recommend Netlify or Vercel — both have free plans that work great for small sites).

What if I need changes after launch?

Standard and Custom packages include 30 days of free tweaks after launch. After that, ongoing support is available at an hourly rate, or as a small monthly retainer if you want regular updates.

How do payments work?

50% upfront to start the project, 50% on launch. Payments via UPI, bank transfer, or PayPal. You get a proper invoice for each payment.

Do you only build with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript?

That's our default — it makes sites fast, easy to host, and easy to maintain. For projects that need a CMS (so you can edit content yourself), we can also work with WordPress or headless options. Just ask.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes — we work with clients globally over email and WhatsApp. We accept international payments via PayPal or bank transfer, and we'll match our working hours to overlap with yours where it helps.

Can you redesign or rebuild an existing site?

Definitely. We can redesign a tired site, rebuild a slow one for speed, or migrate you off a bloated builder (Wix, Squarespace, etc.) onto clean code you fully own. Share your current URL when you email and we'll send back honest thoughts before quoting.
