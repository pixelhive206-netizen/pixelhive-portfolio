# Pixelhive Outreach Playbook — Foreign Client Acquisition

A no-cold-calling system for closing $2,500–$5,000 deals from US/UK/AU small businesses.

Built around three principles:
1. **Async, not phone** — written outreach, video audits, email-first communication
2. **Niche, then scale** — start with restaurants OR clinics, get 2 wins, expand
3. **Give value before asking** — free Loom audit is the door-opener

Expected math: **100 personalized emails → 8–15 replies → 3–5 calls → 1 closed deal at $3,500.**

---

## Phase 1 — Find your first 50 leads (1 evening of work)

### Source A — Google Maps / Yelp / Yellow Pages

Search for `[business type] in [city]` in the country you want to target. Example searches:

- `restaurants in Brighton UK`
- `dental clinics in Portland Oregon`
- `boutique hotels in Edinburgh`
- `coffee shops in Austin TX`
- `physiotherapy clinics in Melbourne`

Look for businesses that:
- Have **no website** (just Facebook / Instagram / Google Maps listing)
- Have a **broken / outdated** website (Flash, no mobile version, last updated 2014)
- Have a **slow Wix or Squarespace** site
- Have a **template-heavy WordPress** site with the wrong fonts/colors

For each lead, save in a spreadsheet:
- Business name
- Owner / decision-maker name (LinkedIn search)
- Their current website URL (or "no website")
- Public email (from their site, Facebook, Google Maps)
- One personalized observation about their business

### Source B — Reddit, Facebook Groups, Indie Hackers

Look for posts where business owners ask:
- "My website is slow, what should I do?"
- "Looking for a web designer in [city]"
- "How much should I pay for a website?"

Reply helpfully (not pitchy). Build authority. They'll DM you.

### Source C — Awwwards-style scraping

Some businesses get featured on local "best of" lists. They've shown they care about quality, low-hanging fruit.

## Phase 3 — The Loom audit (your door-opener)

When they reply "yes", you have **24 hours** to send the audit. Don't let it go cold.

### What to record (4 minutes max)

Use **Loom** (free at loom.com/screen-recorder) — screen + face cam in corner. Their face cam in corner builds trust.

**Structure:**

| Time | What you say |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | "Hi [Name], thanks for the reply. I'm Anish from Pixelhive Studio. I spent 15 minutes looking at [Site]. Here's what I noticed." |
| 0:15–1:00 | Run **PageSpeed Insights** on their site live on camera. Show the score. Say "see, your mobile score is 38, which means most visitors are seeing this..." (show the slow-loading) |
| 1:00–2:00 | Click through 2–3 pages. Point out specific issues: "this hero image is 4 MB, it should be 200 KB", "the menu link on mobile is hidden behind a hamburger that doesn't work on iPhone Safari", "this CTA is below the fold on mobile" |
| 2:00–3:00 | Show one example of what "right" looks like — quickly demo [maramthetree.com](https://maramthetree.com) loading in under 2 seconds. "This is what your site could feel like." |
| 3:00–3:45 | "Three things I'd fix, in order of impact: 1) Compress your hero image (saves 3.8s of load time). 2) Move the booking CTA above the fold on mobile. 3) Add a proper mobile menu." |
| 3:45–4:00 | "I'm not pitching you on a rebuild yet — these three things are quick wins anyone can do. But if you want a fresh, faster site, I build them for $3,500–$5,000 and deliver in 2 weeks. Reply if you want to chat. Otherwise, hope this was useful. Cheers." |

### Tone notes

- **Don't sell.** Give value, then mention pricing in one line at the very end.
- **Speak slowly.** Cinema-level pace. They're paying attention.
- **No filler words.** Cut "um" and "you know" in your head.
- **Smile** — the corner camera shows your face. People buy from smiling humans.

## Phase 4 — Follow-up sequence

If no reply within 4 days, send Touch 2.

### Touch 2 — Day 4

**Subject:** RE: [original subject]

**Body:**

> Hi [Name] — sending this back to the top of your inbox in case it got buried.
>
> Did the audit video reach you?
>
> Anish

### Touch 3 — Day 11

**Subject:** Last note — [Business Name]

**Body:**

> Hey [Name], no worries if the timing isn't right. Closing the loop on my side.
>
> If a new site ever moves up your priority list, you know where to find me. Site's at pixelhivestudio.in if it helps.
>
> All the best,
> Anish

After Touch 3, **stop**. Don't be a pest. Move on. A "no" today might be a "yes" 6 months from now if you didn't burn the bridge.

## Phase 5 — When they say "yes, let's talk"

### The discovery call (30 min)

Book via **Calendly** (free) — share a link in the email reply.

**Agenda:**

| Time | Topic |
|---|---|
| 0–5 min | Their business: what they do, who their customers are, what they want from the site |
| 5–15 min | Their pain: what's broken, what they've tried, what's working |
| 15–25 min | Your approach: hand-coded, fast, 2 weeks, examples — share screen + walk through maramthetree.com |
| 25–28 min | Pricing: clear, written, no negotiation. "Standard package is $3,500–$5,000 for what you're describing. 50% upfront, 50% on launch." |
| 28–30 min | Next steps: "I'll send a written proposal in 24 hours. If it looks right, sign + 50% deposit and we start next week." |

## Mindset reset

You'll send 100 emails and 92 won't reply. **This is normal.** Don't take it personally. The 8 replies are why you do it.

After the first $3,500 deal closes, the entire system pays for itself for the next 6 months. After the second, you're in profit. After the fifth, you're picking and choosing.

Stay patient. Stay async. Stay generous with the free value.
